FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is Riskr non-custodial and secure?

Yes. Riskr does not store, request, or have access to your wallet private keys or recovery phrases. All matches and fund escrows occur through verified on-chain SPL-Token transfers and automated smart settlements.

What happens in case of a Draw in RPS Combat?

In RPS Combat, if both host and challenger play the same hand (e.g. Rock vs Rock), the duel resolves as DRAW. Both players receive a 100% full refund of their stake directly back to their wallet, with 0% protocol fee charged.

What wallets are compatible with Riskr?

Riskr connects seamlessly with Phantom, Solflare, and the Solana Mobile Saga Seed Vault via the official Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA). Private keys never leave your secure enclave.

How does the Token Burn Incinerator work?

The Incinerator sends SPL tokens to Solana's unrecoverable protocol incinerator address (1nc1nerator...1111) using Opcode 8 Burn instructions. Burned tokens are permanently removed from circulating supply, increasing deflationary pressure.
